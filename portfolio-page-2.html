My Second Project

Orange paint textured background abstract painting

This was my second painting. I began working on it in January 2020 and finished it in in April of the same year;

I was inspired by a sudden rush of emotion—something between chaos and joy—and let that guide my brush with no clear subject in mind. I reached for fiery oranges, soft whites, and grounded greens to reflect that emotional intensity and contrast.

The colors weren’t planned; they just felt right in the moment, like feelings choosing their own language. Each layer and swirl became a kind of rhythm, a visual expression of energy I couldn’t put into words.
