I create...

Conceptual Portraiture

I create artworks that fuse imagination with meaning — from symbolic portraits that explore identity and memory to narrative scenes filled with subtle social commentary. Every stroke serves a thought, emotion, or story.

Abstract Botanical Art

Inspired by nature’s organic forms, I paint floral and botanical abstractions that pulse with energy. These works capture not just how nature looks, but how it feels — blooming, swirling, layered, and alive. My botanical art is both decorative and emotional, rooted in texture and movement

Expressive Art

I create textured, emotionally rich works that channel mood, memory, and motion. Each piece is a visual response to the world—sometimes chaotic, sometimes serene—but always alive. My expressive art uses bold color, layered textures, and movement to evoke feeling beyond words

A little about me...

I’m an African woman and visual artist whose work explores identity, memory, and emotion through expressive color, symbolism, and natural forms. Blending conceptual portraiture with abstract botanical and expressive art, I paint stories that speak beyond words.
